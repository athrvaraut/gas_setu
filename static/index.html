The five outcomes
CLEARNothing to note. The booking proceeds.
MONITORLogged. Nobody needs to act on this booking.
REVIEWThe booking proceeds and a person looks at the case. This is the strongest outcome the consumption model can cause.
HOLD_FOR_REVIEWWaits for a person, within 24 hours. Commercial bookings only, when the business identity fails verification.
CONVERT_TO_COMMERCIALAn active GSTIN for a food business is registered at the delivery address: a commercial cylinder is offered. The customer can appeal.

Measured on simulated data
No labelled LPG fraud data is public, so the consumption model is tested on a simulator built from published averages. With reviewers able to look at 0.5% of connections, in ten train/test worlds:
Share of reviewed cases that were real diversion · mean (range) over 10 seeds · experiments/consumption_eval.py · not a measurement of real fraud
⚠️
What this prototype cannot do
It has no real booking data. A pilot with an oil company or a distributor is needed before any claim about real fraud.
A business that never declares a GSTIN is found only by its name. Address-based discovery is planned.
A household that sells part of its own quota looks like any heavy user. The model finds few of these.
Cheaters who spread their bookings out are harder to find; the experiments measure how much.
